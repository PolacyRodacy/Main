Overview

The Brawl Stars Amateur League is an amateur Brawl Stars league featuring ten invited teams. The league follows a single round-robin format, with every team playing against every other team once.

Format

Ten teams are directly invited to the league. There are no qualification stages.

  • 10 invited teams
  • 9 Match Weeks
  • 9 matches per team
  • Single round-robin

Point System

Every point for every set won.

If don't arrive, team get -3 points.
